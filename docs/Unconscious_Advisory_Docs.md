## 5. Competitor Analysis Strategy

### Competitive Landscape Context
The commercial market is flooded with two extremes: automated software platforms that generate raw heatmaps/AI recommendations without context, and enterprise neuroscience labs that deliver slow, academic reports. Your practice occupies the high-margin white space between them.

**Competitor Evaluation Dimensions:**
1. **Positioning & Framing:** What is their core thesis? Who is their buyer? Do they use excessive jargon?
2. **Productization & Pricing Mechanics:** What is their entry wedge? How cleanly do they boundary their work (deliverables vs. outcomes)? Are prices transparent?
3. **Proof & Authority Signaling:** Do case studies focus on methodology or commercial revenue? Where do they publish thought leadership?
4. **Strategic Advantage:** Translating complexity into actionable business strategy instead of just delivering raw dashboards.

**Strategic Advantages over Competitors:**
- High-Velocity Delivery: 8+ years of expert pattern recognition delivers faster answers without tech bloat.
- Gain-Framed Commercial Focus: "Planning for growth" outperforms loss-framed or academic approaches.
- Executive Advisory Depth: Translating complexity into actionable business strategy instead of just delivering raw dashboards.
