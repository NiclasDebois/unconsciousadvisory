# Unconscious Advisory - Brand Strategy & One-Pager Overview

## Executive Manifesto & Positioning

### Core Slogan
"Planning for the unconscious is planning for growth."

### Core Purpose & Value Proposition
Most commercial campaigns underperform in the first 1,000 milliseconds—not because the core message is flawed, but due to visual scanning path misalignment, sub-optimal brand placement, or channel context resistance.

Unconscious Advisory leverages 8+ years of proprietary biometric research and expert pattern recognition to provide direct strategic evaluation, align commercial creative with non-conscious human behavior, and protect media capital before launch.

### Core Strategic Foundations & Rules
1. **The Commercial Anchor Rule:** Always anchor "unconscious" immediately to commercial strategy ("Unconscious Advisory", "Non-Conscious Commercial Strategy") to differentiate from generic HR training.
2. **Gain-Framed Growth Architecture:** Never use loss-framed or defensive defect terms (*Mismatch*, *Overload*, *Failure*, *Defect*). Consistently enforce gain-framed accelerators (*Alignment*, *Capacity Optimization*, *Anchoring*, *Balance*).
3. **Prescriptive Action Over Passive Data:** Deliver direct visual hierarchy blueprints and text rewrites—never passive heatmaps or uncontextualized raw data dumps.
4. **Intake Uniformity:** All intake and scheduling CTAs strictly route to the official [Google Booking Schedule](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/appointments/schedules/AcZssZ2LcS4_7GxVLhhBL3vSK7lkXRTVE4_ukA2afldgJuEk6k5xiYzDeJOOY-fLtUwEzAK8qryxdggb).
