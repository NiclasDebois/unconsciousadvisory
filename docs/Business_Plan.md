# Unconscious Advisory - Strategic Business Plan

## 1. Practice Overview & Executive Manifesto

**Principal:** Niclas Debois  
**Core Thesis:** "Planning for the unconscious is planning for growth."  
**Positioning:** High-Velocity Strategic Advisory & Pre-Launch Capital Protection at the intersection of applied cognitive science, consumer psychology, attention architecture, and commercial performance strategy.

### Core Value Proposition
Most commercial campaigns underperform in the first 1,000 milliseconds due to sub-optimal visual scanning paths, late brand placement, or platform context resistance. Unconscious Advisory bridges the gap between scientific rigor and commercial velocity. Grounded in 8+ years of proprietary biometric research and expert pattern recognition, the practice optimizes commercial strategy and aligns creative assets with non-conscious human behavior before media capital is deployed.

### Brand Governance & Operating Constraints
1. **Commercial Anchor Enforced:** Never use "Unconscious" in public copy without an immediate commercial anchor ("Unconscious Advisory", "Non-Conscious Commercial Strategy").
2. **Banned Terminology:** Purge the words "Audit" and "Diagnostic" from all client-facing copy and service descriptions. Use "Optimization Sprint", "Strategic Evaluation", or "Performance Assessment".
3. **Gain-Framed Language:** Ban loss-framed or defensive defect terms (*Mismatch*, *Overload*, *Failure*, *Defect*). Consistently apply gain-framed growth accelerators (*Alignment*, *Capacity Optimization*, *Anchoring*, *Balance*).
4. **Vendor Neutrality:** Attribute all methodology strictly to "8+ years of proprietary biometric research and expert pattern recognition." Never reference third-party software platforms or vendor names.
5. **Intake Uniformity:** All intake and booking CTAs strictly route to the official [Google Booking Schedule](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/appointments/schedules/AcZssZ2LcS4_7GxVLhhBL3vSK7lkXRTVE4_ukA2afldgJuEk6k5xiYzDeJOOY-fLtUwEzAK8qryxdggb).
6. **No-Agency Boundary:** Stay strictly within high-margin strategic advisory; never expand into media buying, copywriting, or generic BI dashboard maintenance.
